How can we help?

Welcome to the Palatty Rosary support page. Find answers to common questions about using the app on your iPhone.

How do I pray the Rosary?

Open Palatty Rosary, select the Rosary, and follow the prayers and bead-by-bead navigation on screen.

How do I use Kids Mode?

Use the Kids Mode toggle on the home screen to switch to the child-friendly presentation.

How does the Catholic Calendar work?

Open Calendar to view selected Catholic observances and your prayer completion history. A day receives one checkmark when you finish the Rosary.

Can I listen to prayers?

Where available, tap the Listen control next to a prayer. Voice options may vary by device and language.

Why won't a video or daily reading load?

Some content requires an internet connection and may depend on third-party availability. Try reconnecting to the internet and reopening the section.
